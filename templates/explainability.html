Domain Findings Derived from Model Attribution

Soil Saturation Dominance

The top 3 risk drivers across Odisha are consistently Rainfall_Prev_7d_Sum, Rainfall_Prev_15d_Sum, and Rainfall_Prev_30d_Sum. Single-day spikes without prior ground saturation rarely generate severe riverine inundations.

Wet Spell Duration Effect

Consecutive_Rainy_Days_Before acts as a critical multiplier. When 4+ consecutive rain days precede a 70mm storm, runoff velocity accelerates due to near-zero infiltration capacity in coastal alluvium.

Peak Monsoon Seasonality

Month_cos and Month_sin cyclical variables naturally weight late July, August, and early September as the peak state vulnerability window, matching the historical trajectory of Bay of Bengal cyclonic depressions.
