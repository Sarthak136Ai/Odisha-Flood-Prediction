Spatial Grid Refinement

To bridge the spatial resolution gap between coarse gridded satellite/reanalysis datasets and local rain gauge telemetry across Odisha's 354 blocks, a dedicated gradient-boosted statistical downscaling model (HistGradientBoostingRegressor) was trained and evaluated on 24 years of telemetry.

Key Scientific Findings

  • 1. Extreme Value Preservation: The downscaling engine successfully captures localized convective peak precipitation (>100 mm/day) without severe dampening or artificial smoothing.
  • 2. Stable Downstream ROC-AUC: Feeding downscaled inputs into the frozen flood prediction pipeline achieves a Test ROC-AUC of 0.9703 (vs 0.9693 observed), demonstrating high spatial robustness.
  • 3. Operational Feasibility: Enables reliable block-level risk forecasting even when only coarse-grid regional meteorological inputs are available.
