Grounding & Architecture

  • Interface Layer: The assistant acts as an interpretive interface layer rather than the core machine learning model.
  • Deterministic Grounding: Queries route directly to verified model pipelines (FloodPredictor), metadata, and historical telemetry records without inventing predictions.
  • Missing Data Transparency: If data or models are unavailable for a requested station, date, or query, the system explicitly reports that sufficient project data is not available.
  • Operational Disclaimer: Predictions provide advisory risk estimates for disaster management and do not supersede statutory warnings from IMD or SRC.
