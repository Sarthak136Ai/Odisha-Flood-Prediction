End-to-End System Methodology & Dual-Pipeline Architecture

State-Wide Multi-Station Pipeline
1. Historical Benchmark Pipeline (2001–2024)
1. Raw Telemetry Ingestion: 24 Annual CSVs (2001.csv – 2024.csv), 2,752,252 daily station observations across 30 districts.
2. Feature Engineering & Quality Audit: Lagged precipitation (1d, 2d, 3d, 7d), cumulative rolling sums/max (3d, 7d, 15d, 30d), antecedent wet spell counts, cyclical month harmonics. Zero data leakage verified.
3. Chronological Temporal Partition: Train: 2001–2018 (75%), Validation: 2019–2021 (12.5%), Independent Test: 2022–2024 (12.5%).
4. Model Benchmark & Calibration: Evaluated Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forest, XGBoost, and ANN. Calibrated optimal threshold (0.8345) to balance precision/recall under class imbalance.
5. Model Freezing: Weights, threshold (0.8345), and feature schema frozen in best_model.pkl.
2. Operational Unseen Inference Pipeline (2025)
1. Unseen Operational Ingestion: data/raw/operational/2025.csv (114,610 daily records, unobserved ground truth).
2. Schema & District Validation: Strictly verifies 30 standard district names, ISO dates, null values, and enforces target guardrail (no fabricated flood label).
3. Dec 2024 Warm-Up Context Stitching: Stitches preceding 35 days of December 2024 rainfall to prevent rolling window boundary zero-fill distortions.
4. Frozen Model Inference & SHAP Drivers: Applies frozen model to estimate flood probabilities, classify risk tiers (Low, Moderate, High), and isolate top 3 risk factors.
5. Independent Retrospective Engine: Evaluates future SRC ground truth without ever retraining or modifying the frozen operational model.
